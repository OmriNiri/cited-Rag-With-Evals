# Judge check — blind human labels

Label each answer with the same rubric the judge uses, using ONLY the quotes (not your own knowledge of the law):

- `supported`: every statement in the answer, including a yes/no conclusion, is directly stated in the quotes.
- `partial`: some statements are stated in the quotes, but at least one statement or the conclusion is not.
- `unsupported`: the main statement or conclusion is not stated in the quotes, or contradicts them.

Write one word after "Your verdict:". Do not look at eval/results/ before you are done.

## q01

**Question:** What is the notice period after the probation period has ended?

**Answer:** The notice period is one month during the first year of service, two months in the second to ninth years of service, and three months thereafter.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 335c) "The employment relationship may be terminated at one month’s notice during the first year of service, at two months’ notice in the second to ninth years of service and at three months’ notice thereafter,"

Your verdict: supported

## q06

**Question:** During which periods may the employer not terminate the employment relationship after the probation period?

**Answer:** During the pregnancy of an employee and the sixteen weeks following birth, and between the start of leave in accordance with Article 329f paragraph 3 and the last day of leave taken, but for a maximum of three months from the end of the period in accordance with letter c.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 336c) "during the pregnancy of an employee and the sixteen weeks following birth;"
2. (Art. 336c) "between the start of leave in accordance with Article 329f paragraph 3 and the last day of leave taken, but for a maximum of three months from the end of the period in accordance with letter c;"

Your verdict: supported

## q10

**Question:** How long is the probation period and what notice applies during it?

**Answer:** The probation period is the first month of an employment relationship and may not exceed three months. During the probation period, either party may terminate the contract at any time by giving seven days’ notice.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 335b) "During the probation period, either party may terminate the contract at any time by giving seven days’ notice; the probation period is considered to be the first month of an employment relationship."

Your verdict: partial

## q12

**Question:** Do I still get paid if I can't work because I broke my leg?

**Answer:** If you have compulsory insurance prescribed by law against the financial consequences of being prevented from working by personal circumstances for which you are not at fault, the employer is not obliged to pay your salary where the insurance benefits for that limited period cover at least four-fifths of the salary income lost over that period.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 324b) "If the employee has compulsory insurance prescribed by law against the financial consequences of being prevented from working by personal circumstances for which he is not at fault,"

Your verdict: partial

## q19

**Question:** What happens to the employment relationship when the employer transfers the company to a third party?

**Answer:** The employment relationship and all attendant rights and obligations pass to the acquirer as of the day of the transfer, unless the employee refuses such transfer.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 333) "Where the employer transfers the company or a part thereof to a third party, the employment relationship and all attendant rights and obligations pass to the acquirer as of the day of the transfer, unless the employee refuses such transfer."

Your verdict: supported

## q21

**Question:** What is the maximum compensation for unlawful termination of the employment relationship?

**Answer:** six months’ salary for the employee

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 336a) "The court determines the compensation taking due account of all the circumstances, though it must not exceed an amount equivalent to six months’ salary for the employee."

Your verdict: supported

## q23

**Question:** When may the employment relationship be terminated with immediate effect for good cause?

**Answer:** Both employer and employee may terminate the employment relationship with immediate effect at any time for good cause.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 337) "Both employer and employee may terminate the employment relationship with immediate effect at any time for good cause;"

Your verdict: supported

## q25

**Question:** Can my old employer stop me from working for a competitor for five years?

**Answer:** No, the prohibition of competition may exceed three years only in special circumstances.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 340a) "The prohibition must be appropriately restricted with regard to place, time and scope such that it does not unfairly compromise the employee’s future economic activity; it may exceed three years only in special circumstances."

## q29

**Question:** How many days off can I take to look after my sick partner?

**Answer:** No more than three days per event and no more than ten days per year.

**Quotes:**

1. (Art. 329h) "An employee is entitled to paid leave for the time he or she spends caring for a family member or life partner with health problems; however, the leave is limited to no more than three days per event and no more than ten days per year."

Your verdict: supported
